# Tone of Voice — Three Pillars

Inspira has one voice with three facets. Every piece of copy should register in at least one pillar. The best copy blends all three.

## 1. Visionária

Sees beyond the present. Leads transformation. Challenges the status quo. Proposes new ways of working.

The future is not distant — it's a space we create and evolve.

**Use when:** introducing new capabilities, framing product direction, closing onboarding, rallying around possibility.

**Feels like:** "Sempre à frente: construindo o futuro da prática jurídica."

## 2. Colaborativa

Co-creates. Listens. Builds relationships. Proximity over distance. Partnership over transaction.

Inspira is a teammate, not a vendor.

**Use when:** explaining shared work, writing empty states, onboarding, messages that invite action.

**Feels like:** "IA como aliada na construção da sua prática jurídica."

## 3. Expert

Confident. Clear. Authoritative — but curious, humble, generous. Ready to teach AND to learn.

Authority without arrogance. Expertise that invites, not excludes.

**Use when:** documentation, error messages, field labels, help text, anything that must be precise.

**Feels like:** "A tecnologia desenvolvida por e para advogados: simples, precisa e eficaz."

---

## How pillars combine

Any Inspira touchpoint should scan in at least one pillar. The strongest copy scans in two or three.

| Pillar(s)             | Good for                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visionária alone      | Marketing moments, new feature announcements, vision statements              |
| Colaborativa alone    | Empty states, invitations, friendly confirmations                            |
| Expert alone          | Error messages, tooltips, field hints, documentation                         |
| Visionária + Expert   | Product launches, capability intros ("O que está pronto, e para onde vamos") |
| Colaborativa + Expert | Onboarding flows, guided experiences, help content                           |
| All three             | Flagship moments: homepage hero, launch posts, brand film                    |

## What Inspira is NOT

- Not flippant. No "rapidinha," "né?!", or forced casualness.
- Not rigid. No legalese when plain Portuguese works.
- Not anonymous. We say "nós" — Inspira shows up as a collective.
- Not salesy. We invite, we don't push.
