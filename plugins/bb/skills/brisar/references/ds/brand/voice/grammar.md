# Grammar & Copy Rules

These rules apply to every Inspira surface: product UI, marketing, documentation, error messages, onboarding, emails. When in doubt, follow these — they're not suggestions.

## Gender

- Inspira is **feminine**: "Somos **a** Inspira," "a ferramenta."
- Never "o produto" or "o Inspira."

## Self-reference

- Use **"nós / nosso"** — Inspira speaks as a collective.
- "Atuamos para..." ✅ — not "Inspira atua para..." ❌
- "Nossos recursos..." ✅ — not "Os recursos da Inspira..." ❌

## What Inspira calls itself

- **"Ferramenta"** ✅ — not "plataforma" ❌ (too generic, too SaaS).

## Register

- Formal but **not legalese**. Plain Portuguese beats Latin phrases.
- Serious but **accessible**. No jargon for its own sake.
- No colloquialisms: avoid "rapidinha," "né?!", "tipo assim."
- No exclamation stacking: one `!` per thought, max.

## Voice

- **Active voice preferred.** Inspira is the agent, the protagonist.
- "Nossos recursos simplificam sua rotina" ✅
- "A rotina é simplificada pelos recursos" ❌

## Verbal mood

- **Indicativo** (for confidence): "Simplificamos sua busca."
- **Imperativo** (for expert tone): "Comece pelo documento principal."
- **Avoid subjunctive** when indicative works. Confidence over hedging.

## Tense

- **Present tense preferred.**
- "Nossos recursos simplificam sua rotina" ✅ — not "simplificarão" ❌
- Future tense only when genuinely future.

## Referring to people

- **Client's team:** "sua equipe" / "seu time"
- **Our team:** "nosso time" (never "colaboradores")
- **Leaders:** "lideranças" (never "líderes" or "diretores")
- **Job titles:** "Pessoa Coordenadora," "Pessoa Desenvolvedora"

## Gender-inclusive language

- **Feminine first** in paired references: "advogadas e advogados."
- **Neutral alternatives** when paired form is heavy:
  - "profissionais do Direito"
  - "equipe jurídica"
- **Non-binary reference:** "pessoa advogada."
- **Avoid masculine plurals** that erase women:
  - "estamos muito felizes" ✅ — not "estamos animados" ❌
- **Never use `@` or `x`**: no "bem-vind@s," no "amigxs."
- **Prefer neutral paths** that sidestep the problem:
  - "Boas-vindas! Que bom ter você conosco!" — no gender marker required.

## Capitalization

| Term | Form |
|---|---|
| Direito (the field) | Uppercase **D** |
| jurídico / jurídica | Lowercase |
| inteligência artificial | Lowercase |
| IA | Uppercase |
| Inspira | Initial cap always |

## Key brand terms

- **"Inspire-se"** — the conceptual CTA / invitation. Use it when inviting exploration: "inspire-se no futuro da advocacia."
- **"Prática jurídica"** — the umbrella term for the entire legal sector. Prefer this over "mercado jurídico" or "área jurídica."

## Error and microcopy specifics

- **Name the problem.** Not "Algo deu errado." Instead: "Não conseguimos salvar suas alterações."
- **Name the next step.** Every error tells the user what to do: "Tente novamente em instantes" / "Verifique sua conexão."
- **Destructive confirmations name the thing.** "Apagar 3 documentos?" not "Tem certeza?"
- **Empty states teach.** Don't just say "Sem resultados" — explain what to try next.

## Quick check before shipping copy

1. Does it use feminine for Inspira? ("a ferramenta")
2. Does it use "nós" when Inspira refers to itself?
3. Is it in present tense, active voice, indicative/imperative mood?
4. Does it avoid legalese without becoming casual?
5. If it names people, is it gender-inclusive without `@` or `x`?
6. If it's an error or empty state, does it name the problem AND the next step?
